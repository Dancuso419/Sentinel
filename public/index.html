Every report gets a number. Every number keeps its trail.

Sentinel is a computerized crime reporting and tracking system. File an incident with or without an account, get a case ID on the spot, and check what happened to it without going back to a station.

A case ID is the whole mechanism.

Paper reports go into a ledger and stop there. Here, the number you are given is a live handle on the record — and every hand that touches it is written down.

  1. 01

    You file, the system numbers it

    A report submitted without an account stores no identity at all — and still returns a CR-YYYY-NNNN case ID. Anonymity and follow-up usually trade off. Here they don't.

  2. 02

    An officer moves it forward

    Cases run pending → investigating → resolved, and never backward. Each transition writes a row naming who changed it and when.

  3. 03

    You read the trail back

    Type the case ID into the panel beside this and the status comes straight from the database. That is the whole answer to "what happened to my report?"

Who is working these cases.

Officers are ranked by resolutions the reporter did not reject — not by how many cases they close. Closing a lot of cases badly does not move anyone up this board.

Loading the board…

Score is cases resolved minus the resolutions the person who reported them said were not resolved. Average close is the mean days from a report being filed to it being closed. Published because a person deciding whether to report at all is entitled to see whether resolutions here hold up.

Two ways in. Pick the one that fits your situation.

Report without an account

Nothing is stored that points back to you — no name, no email, no citizen record. You are shown a case ID once, on screen. Write it down; it cannot be recovered.

Start an anonymous report

Report with an account

Your reports collect on one dashboard, you can correct or withdraw them while they are still pending, and you get a banner the moment a status changes.

Create a citizen account

Keep the case ID. It is the only key.

For a walk-in report there is no account to log back into and no way to look the number up again. Screenshot it, write it on paper, send it to yourself — before you close the page.
